Throughout my internship and learning journey in a corporate environment, participating in technology events, workshops, and hackathons has played a crucial role in bridging the gap between academic theory and practical production. These events are not only opportunities to update the most advanced technological trends in Cloud Computing, Generative AI, and Agentic Systems, but also a chance for me to learn real-world problem-solving mindsets, project scope management, and infrastructure optimization techniques from experienced experts and engineers. Below is a summary of 3 typical and most memorable events I have attended:

### [Event 1: First Cloud AI Journey Meetup: Cloud, Data & DevOps Career Journey](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** First Cloud AI Journey Meetup: Cloud, Data & DevOps Career Journey

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00, June 13, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Building, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in Event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief Description:** The event gathered multiple industry speakers, focusing on sharing the practical picture of roles such as Data Analytics Engineer, DevOps Engineer, and Cloud Specialist in enterprises and multinational corporations (MNCs).

&emsp;**Results & Achievements:** 
* Gained a clearer understanding of the overall landscape and market requirements for Data Analytics Engineer, DevOps Engineer, and Cloud Specialist positions.
* Gathered valuable advice on career development roadmaps, core skills required, and how to adapt to working environments in large corporations.

---

### [Event 2: Cloud Architect Game Show](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Cloud Architect Game Show

&emsp;**Time:** June 20, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in Event:** Attendee / Head-to-Head Audience Observer

&emsp;**Brief Description:** The event was organized as a direct head-to-head game show format among competing teams, revolving around solving and analyzing question sets and architecture scenarios based on standard AWS certifications from Foundational, Associate, to Professional & Specialty levels.

&emsp;**Results & Achievements:** 
* Solidified core knowledge of the *Shared Responsibility Model*, compute and storage services, as well as high-availability system design thinking (Multi-AZ, Auto Scaling, Load Balancer) and system decoupling (SQS, SNS).
* Enhanced *Trade-off Thinking* among Cost, Reliability, and Performance when building Cloud architectures.

### [Event 3: Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon](4.3-Event3/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon

&emsp;**Time:** July 25, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in Event:** Attendee / Practical Solution Presentation Listener

&emsp;**Brief Description:** The event brought together teams presenting practical Artificial Intelligence applications, ranging from an intelligent food ordering system via Zalo/WhatsApp, a business strategy analysis tool (Signal Scout), an automated system architecture design assistant (Plan V), a real-time crowd security monitoring system (3KA), to an automated anti-money laundering (AML) workflow engine.

&emsp;**Results & Achievements:** 
* Captured a comprehensive overview of how to apply Generative AI, Sub-agents, Computer Vision (YOLO), and Serverless architecture on AWS to enterprise real-world problems.
* Gained a deeper understanding of the importance of Human-in-the-Loop mindset, project scope management (*Scope Management*), and Cloud infrastructure cost optimization techniques.
