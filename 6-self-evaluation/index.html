Self-Assessment

During my internship at Amazon Web Services (AWS) under the FCAJ Program from 2026-06-01 to 2026-06-15, I had the invaluable opportunity to bridge the gap between the Computer Science foundational theories acquired at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT) and a dynamic, enterprise-grade cloud computing environment.

I actively participated in building a standard Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system. Through this hands-on project, I significantly sharpened my technical skills in cloud infrastructure provisioning, understanding and deploying core AWS services, system architecture design, API integration, …. I also gained practical experience in balancing system performance with cost optimization.

In terms of work ethic, I consistently strived to deliver high-quality, secure code, strictly complied with AWS security protocols and workplace regulations, and proactively engaged with senior cloud engineers to optimize overall team efficiency.

Despite the above achievements, there are several areas identified for improvement:
